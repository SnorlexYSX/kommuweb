Welcome to Kommunity.

Highway commutes, stop-and-go city traffic, long road trips, the same school or office run — tiring drives most of us take every day. In Malaysia that's KL traffic on a weekday, or bumper-to-bumper on the highway when everyone's heading home for the festivities.

That's why we built KommuAssist 2 — not to change the car you love, but to make those drives easier. AI-powered cruise for a more relaxed, safer long-hour trip. Lane keeping when your focus wears thin after a long day. Same car, same roads — a calmer ride when you need it.

At the heart of it is bukapilot, an open-source AI driving model built upon openpilot and trained on 100M+ miles of real-world driving from a global community. You can see it on the road: Kommunity members log more than 360,000 minutes every month — 6.77 million km to date — and each journey helps the stack get a little sharper for everyone.

Whether you're curious, already driving with us, or just watching from the sidelines — we're glad you're here.
