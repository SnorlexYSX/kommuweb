KommuAssist 2 is an AI-powered level 2 driver assistance system — you stay in control, but the system shares the workload on routes you drive every week.

What it helps with

AI autocruise — maintains speed and following distance on highways and fast roads
Active lane keep — helps keep you centred with always active steering support, follows lead car, detects border, even faded line markings
Traffic-aware behaviour — smoother responses in stop-and-go congestion
Road understanding (beta) — tuned for curves, traffic lights, and speed bumps
Driver attentiveness monitoring — ensures you remain attentive while assist is active without hands on wheel alert
